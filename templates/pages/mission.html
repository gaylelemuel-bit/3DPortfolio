About Me
My Story & Mission

I am a faith-centered software developer who believes technology is one of the most powerful tools God has given this generation — and it must be used intentionally. Hosea 4:6 is a constant reminder that knowledge matters, and that ignorance has consequences. My mission is to close that gap: building digital tools that educate, empower, and uplift communities rooted in faith.

Whether it's a Web Application, Troubleshooting, or a Data Pipeline, I approach every project as an act of service — to God first, and then to the people who will use what I build.
